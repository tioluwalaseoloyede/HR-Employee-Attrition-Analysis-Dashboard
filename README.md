# HR Employee Attrition Analysis Dashboard

## Executive Summary
An end-to-end workforce retention analysis evaluating 1,470 employee records to identify structural attrition drivers, department-level risk factors, and compensation disparities.

## Key Findings & Metrics
- **Overall Turnover:** **16.12%** overall attrition rate (237 employee departures out of 1,470 total staff).
- **Departmental Risk:** **Sales** exhibits the highest turnover rate at **20.63%**, followed by Human Resources (**19.05%**).
- **Overtime Impact:** Employees working overtime experience a **30.53%** turnover rate compared to **10.44%** for non-overtime staff.
- **Tenure Vulnerability:** Staff with under 2 years of tenure account for the largest proportion of resignations.

## Data Structure & Quality
- Includes clean data transformation, coded scale definitions, PivotTable analysis, and interactive executive KPI cards.
